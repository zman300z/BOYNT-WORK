Why BOYNT Exists

BOYNT started with a simple observation: sunglasses and water don't mix very well. Anyone who spends time around lakes, rivers, or the ocean has probably watched a pair of sunglasses slip off their head and disappear beneath the surface. It happens in a split second — and most of the time, they're gone for good.

I wanted to build something better.

BOYNT was created around the idea that sunglasses designed for life around water should actually work in that environment. Instead of sinking the moment they hit the surface, BOYNT sunglasses are designed to float. That small change turns a frustrating, expensive mistake into a quick reach and a laugh.

But BOYNT isn't just about floating frames. It's about building sunglasses that people can actually wear every day. A lot of floating sunglasses look overly sporty or bulky — great for the boat but not something you'd want to wear anywhere else. BOYNT was designed to solve that problem.

Whether you're boating, fishing, paddleboarding, swimming, or just heading out with friends — lightweight frames, polarized UV400 lenses, and durable materials make them comfortable for everyday wear while being tough enough for long days in the sun.

The name BOYNT comes from the idea of buoyancy — staying on the surface instead of disappearing beneath it. It represents the core philosophy of the brand: gear that works with the water, not against it.

At its heart, BOYNT is about freedom around the water and confidence in what you're wearing. No worrying about losing your sunglasses. No babying your gear. Just putting them on in the morning and knowing they're ready for wherever the day takes you.
