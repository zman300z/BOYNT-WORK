How to play

Chess rules apply in full: castling, en passant, promotion, check & checkmate.

Hidden mines are buried on the middle of the board. Move a piece onto a mine and that piece is destroyed (yours or the enemy's).

Minesweeper clues: when a piece lands safely, the square reveals a number = mines touching it (including diagonals). Use it to read the field.

Right-click / long-press a square to plant a 🚩 flag on a suspected mine.

Lose your king to a mine → you lose. Kings are not immune!
